# Machine 1 — Belt Floor Sander (The “Big Machine”)

**Typical Professional Example:** Lägler Hummel® (or equivalent high-end belt sanders)

## Purpose
Primary material-removal machine used for the open field of the floor.  
It strips old finish, levels high spots, removes cupping/crowning, and establishes the flat plane that every subsequent machine builds upon.

## How It Works
- Continuous abrasive belt runs over a cylindrical drum (≈ 200 mm / 8 inches wide).
- Operator walks behind the machine at a steady pace.
- Progressive grit sequence is standard: 36 → 60 → 80/100.
- Drum pressure is adjustable in steps; excessive pressure or stopping creates divots and chatter marks.

## Key Educational Points
- Handles approximately 80 % of total material removal on a typical refinish.
- Incorrect technique leaves waves, side-cut marks, or chatter that later machines struggle to erase.
- Always operated with high-efficiency dust extraction (HEPA or manufacturer bag system).
- At EcoWoods: used exclusively by salaried master artisans; never subcontracted day labour.

## Safety & Skill Notes
Keep the machine moving at all times.  
Stopping or hesitation creates permanent low spots.  
Tracking of the belt and consistent forward speed determine final quality.

## EcoWoods Context
This is the foundation of the mechanical process.  
Mastery of the belt sander determines whether the finished floor will look and perform correctly for decades.
