# Machine 3 — Planetary / Multi-Disc Sander

**Typical Professional Example:** Lägler TRIO® (three independently mounted 200 mm discs)

## Purpose
Refining and blending stage.  
Erases belt lines, edger swirls, and cross-grain scratches while creating a random, multi-directional scratch pattern ideal for even stain and finish absorption.

## How It Works
- Three (or more) counter-rotating discs mounted on a planetary head.
- Discs spin independently while the head itself rotates, producing a non-directional pattern.
- Used after the belt and edger have completed their progressive cuts.
- Excellent for multi-species floors and areas with mixed hard/soft grain.

## Key Educational Points
- Separates a “good” sanding job from a master-level result.
- Ideal for engineered floors and situations where aggressive belt sanding risks sanding through the wear layer.
- Prepares a uniform microscopic surface so stain and finish absorb evenly.
- Modern systems integrate high-efficiency dust extraction.

## Safety & Skill Notes
This is a refining tool, not a primary stock-removal machine.  
Skipping or rushing the planetary pass is one of the most common reasons floors show machine marks after finishing.
