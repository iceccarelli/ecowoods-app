# Machine 4 — Floor Buffer & Screening Machine

**Typical Professional Use:** 16–20 inch single-disc rotary buffer fitted with abrasive screens or multi-disc attachments

## Purpose
Final mechanical preparation of the surface and intercoat abrasion between finish coats.  
Creates a uniform microscopic scratch pattern that allows subsequent finish layers to bond properly.

## How It Works
- Large circular drive plate (typically 16–20 inches / 400–500 mm).
- Fitted with fine mesh screens (usually 100–150 grit) or abrasive pads.
- Light abrasion only — removes microns of material, not wood.
- Used for:
  - Final screening before the first coat of finish
  - Intercoat abrasion between successive coats of water-based or oil finishes

## Key Educational Points
- Last chance to create a perfect substrate for the finish system.
- Mandatory between coats on multi-coat water-based systems (Bona, Loba, Pallmann, etc.).
- Does not correct bad sanding — it only prepares a correctly sanded floor.
- At EcoWoods performed inside the full HEPA dust-containment protocol.

## Safety & Skill Notes
Screening is light abrasion only.  
Excessive pressure or dwell time can burnish the surface or leave residual swirl.  
Keep the machine moving in overlapping passes.

## EcoWoods Context
The buffer enables the low-VOC, GreenGuard Gold finishes specified by EcoWoods.  
It is the final mechanical step that protects the lifetime performance of the floor.
