# Outreach material

Drafts for work that happens off the website. Nothing in this folder is
published by the build — these are documents to send, and every claim in them is
Francisco's to make, not the repository's.

Two rules were applied while writing them, and both are load-bearing:

**1. Every figure is one that is already published on ecowoods.ca.** Price
bands come from `lib/pricing.ts`; the dust-capture figure and the schedule
figures come from `FAQ_ITEMS` and `SERVICES` in `lib/seo-data.ts`; the technique
claims are cited to a paper section. Where a draft would have needed a figure
this site does not publish, the draft says `[CONFIRM: …]` instead of a number.
See `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` for the list.

**2. Nothing asks for a review selectively.** Google's Maps user-contributed
content policy lists, as a prohibited practice, to "discourage or prohibit
negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews from customers." A
template sent only to clients who seemed pleased is that practice. The one here
goes to every completed job.

- `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` — the assertions that need confirming before they can be
  used anywhere, on the site or off it.
- `review-request.md` — email and SMS, sent to every completed job.
- `pitch-01-condo-moisture-and-sound.md`
- `pitch-02-heritage-sanding-and-value.md`
- `pitch-03-oak-specification-and-cost.md`
