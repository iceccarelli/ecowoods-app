**Subject:** The two ways condo hardwood fails in Toronto, and why boards keep
seeing the same complaint

Hi [Name],

Two failure modes account for most hardwood complaints in Toronto towers, and
they are not the ones the flooring ads talk about.

The first is the slab. A concrete floor in a finished unit can still be releasing
moisture years after occupancy, and the test that detects it — in-situ relative
humidity probes, ASTM F2170 — is not the surface reading most installers do. A
floor laid over an untested slab is fine until the first humid August.

The second is the assembly, not the plank. Acoustic performance is a property of
the whole build-up — underlay, adhesive, subfloor — and a board's declaration is
written against the assembly. Buying a plank rated in someone else's assembly
proves nothing about yours.
