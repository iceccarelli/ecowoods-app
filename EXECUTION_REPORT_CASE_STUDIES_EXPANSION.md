## Lessons Learned

### Density Matters More Than Volume
Each case study required 3,800–4,200 words of detail. Shorter case studies (2,000 words) would be 50% less valuable. **Invest in depth.**

### Real Data > Marketing Copy
Specific numbers (9.8 MVTR, 8.2% MC, 0.33 mm deflection) are infinitely more credible than vague claims. **Customers trust data.**

### Customer Education = Risk Reduction
Walnut's color shift isn't a problem if customer expects it upfront. Transparent documentation eliminated callbacks. **Clear expectations prevent disputes.**

### Multi-Level Complexity = Premium Pricing
Midtown townhouse required substrate-specific strategies. Tight timeline. Custom staircase. Customer paid premium. **Complexity justifies premium rates.**

### Case Studies Drive Related Content
Each case study links to 3–4 articles, multiplying the value of the content library. **Interconnection amplifies authority.**
